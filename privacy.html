Privacy Policy

Smashly · Effective October 3, 2026

Smashly does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your devices

Match scores, team names and settings are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Matches scored on Apple Watch are sent directly to your paired iPhone using Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. We never receive this data.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the Apple Watch app reads your heart rate and active energy during a match and saves the match as a workout in Apple Health. Health data stays in Apple Health on your devices, is never sent to us or to any third party, and is never used for advertising.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

Smashly does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
