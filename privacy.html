Privacy Policy

TapCourt · Effective October 6, 2026

TapCourt does not send your match or health data to us, and we do not sell it. The app has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking. Data moves between your own paired devices as described below.

Data stored on your devices

Match scores, team names and settings are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Matches scored on Apple Watch are sent directly to your paired iPhone using Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. We never receive this data.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the Apple Watch app reads your heart rate and active energy during a match and saves the match as a workout in Apple Health. The match record can also include active calories and an average heart-rate summary. That record is stored locally and sent from your Apple Watch to your paired iPhone for match history. These summaries are not confined to the Apple Health app. We do not receive them or use them for advertising. You can change TapCourt’s Health permissions in your device settings. Deleting a match from TapCourt does not delete the separate workout saved in Apple Health; manage that workout in Apple Health.

Apple’s device backup and Health synchronization services are controlled by your Apple account and device settings. No developer-operated server stores your match history.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

TapCourt does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
