TapCourt

Scorekeeper for pickleball, padel, tennis, badminton and table tennis, on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Support

Questions, bug reports or rule requests: email miaoyong2015@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Frequently asked

  • How do I score on Apple Watch? Install TapCourt from the Watch app on your iPhone, open it on the watch, pick a sport and tap the top or bottom half of the screen for the side that won the rally.
  • I bought Pro on my iPhone. Why is the watch still locked? Open TapCourt on the iPhone once while the watch is nearby, or tap “Restore purchase” on the watch.
  • Which rules are supported? Pickleball side-out and rally scoring; padel Star Point, Golden Point and advantage; tennis advantage/no-ad, tiebreaks and match tiebreak; badminton 21-point and BWF 3x15; table tennis to 11.
  • Is Pro a subscription? No. It is a one-time purchase, shared with your Family Sharing group.
