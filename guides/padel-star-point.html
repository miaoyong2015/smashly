The padel Star Point, explained

Since the 2026 season, Premier Padel and FIP events use a new way to settle deuce, called the Star Point.

How the Star Point works

  1. At the first deuce (40-40), play continues with advantage, as in traditional scoring.
  2. If the game returns to deuce, a second advantage is played.
  3. If it reaches deuce a third time, the next point decides the game. That point is the Star Point, and the receiving pair chooses which side receives it.

In short, there can be up to two advantages, and the third deuce is sudden death.

Star Point vs Golden Point vs advantage

FormatWhat happens at 40-40
AdvantagePlay continues until one pair leads by two points.
Golden PointThe next point wins the game, starting from the first deuce.
Star PointTwo advantages are played, then the third deuce is decided by one point.

Sets and tiebreaks

A set goes to 6 games, win by 2. At 6-6, players play a tiebreak to 7, win by 2. In the tiebreak, the first server serves one point, then each player serves two points in turn. Players change ends every 6 points. Many amateur leagues replace the third set with a super tiebreak to 10.

Changing ends

Change ends after the 1st, 3rd, 5th and every odd game of each set.
