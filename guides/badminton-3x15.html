Badminton 3x15 scoring, explained

BWF members voted in April 2026 to switch to a best-of-three-games-to-15 format, which takes effect on 4 January 2027.

The rules at a glance

3x21 (current)3x15 (from 2027)
Points to win a game2115
Win by22
Cap30 (29-29: next point wins)21 (20-20: next point wins)
IntervalWhen the leader reaches 11When the leader reaches 8
MatchBest of 3 gamesBest of 3 games

What stays the same

  • Rally scoring: every rally scores a point, and the winner of the rally serves next.
  • Serve from the right service court when the server's score is even, and from the left when it is odd.
  • In the deciding game, players change ends at the interval.
Ready for both formats. TapCourt supports 21-point games with the 30 cap and the new 3x15 format, and shows which court to serve from.
